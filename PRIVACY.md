# Privacy Policy for SongWeaver

**Last updated: August 2026**

SongWeaver is a local soundboard app. It does not collect, store, transmit, or
share any personal data, usage data, or analytics of any kind.

## What SongWeaver does

- Lets you assign audio files already on your computer to on-screen pads,
  organized into named lists.
- Plays those files back locally when you click a pad or use a keyboard
  shortcut.

## What SongWeaver stores, and where

All data stays on your own device, in a local configuration file next to the
app:

- The names of your pad lists, and which local file path is assigned to each
  pad.
- Your fade/volume/repeat preferences.

None of this is ever uploaded, synced to a server, or shared with anyone,
including the developer. SongWeaver has no account system, no login, and no
network access — it does not make any internet connection at all, other than
the one-time download of the app itself.

## Third parties

SongWeaver does not embed any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, ad
networks, or crash reporters. No data is shared with third parties because
no data leaves your device in the first place.

## Children's privacy

Because SongWeaver collects no data whatsoever, it does not knowingly (or
unknowingly) collect data from children or any other users.

## Changes to this policy

If SongWeaver's behavior around data ever changes, this page will be updated
to reflect that before the change ships.

## Contact
