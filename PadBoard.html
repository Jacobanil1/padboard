Click an empty pad to assign a song from your library · Click a loaded pad to play/stop · Drag & drop an audio file onto a pad also works.
Double-click a song name to rename its pad · ✕ clears a pad · Keys 1-0 and Q-P trigger pads 1-20.
Only one pad plays at a time — starting a new one crossfades out of the current song. Playback keeps going even if you switch lists. Adjust fade timing in ⚙ Settings.
Songs are stored locally in this browser's database — they stay assigned even after closing the app. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
Use the list dropdown to switch pad sets, rename the current one with ✎, or pick + New list… to start a fresh set of 20 pads.
Each list starts with 20 pads — use the − / + above the grid to remove or add a row of 5, from 20 up to 100 per list.
In the player bar, ⏭ Play All auto-plays the next pad's song when one ends, and 🔂 Repeat loops the current song (Repeat wins if both are on).
